About AIDI Uganda

Born from the community, for the community.

AIDI was born in 2026, in a room in Aojakitoi Parish, Kuju Subcounty, Amuria District, where a group of community promoters kept arriving at the same conclusion: the services that Teso depends on are stretched beyond their capacity. The providers exist, they simply cannot reach everyone who needs them. So we decided to become part of the answer.

Who We Are

AIDI is a local, not-for-profit community-based organisation (CBO) in Uganda. We are non-partisan and non-denominational, serving communities regardless of religion, tribe, colour, or race.

Our work is organised around five core objectives and guided by a simple belief: that development works best when it is locally led.

Why We Were Founded

AIDI emerged from a clear-eyed reading of the realities facing our communities. The experience of pandemics and disease, entrenched poverty, harmful cultural beliefs and practices, inadequate service delivery, poor infrastructure, weak agricultural practices, and limited advocacy skills have left many families in situations of extreme deprivation, vulnerability, and displacement. Famine, low skills, and high poverty have become familiar conditions rather than exceptions.

At the same time, the traditional support systems that communities once relied on (community farming support, poverty eradication strategies, capacity-building providers, and lobby and advocacy services) are overwhelmed. They can no longer keep pace with the scale of need. AIDI was constituted to help close that gap.

Our Mission

To mobilise communities for inclusive and collective action in the improvement of poor infrastructure, commercial agriculture enhancement, social inclusion, and improved livelihoods through community engagement and participatory development.

Our Vision

To eradicate poverty in the community.

We work toward thriving communities where families can build secure livelihoods and shape their own development.

Our Goal

To be a leading community-based initiative in promoting infrastructure, sustainable agriculture, climate resilience, poverty eradication, and community empowerment.

What We Do

AIDI's work is organised around five core objectives.

01

Education & Family Capacity

We promote formal and informal educational activities that build families' skills, including adult literacy, vocational education, and support for school infrastructure.

02

Climate-Resilient Agriculture

We build awareness and practices around farming that withstand climate shocks and preserve the land for future generations.

03

Wholesome Community Support

We support society broadly through education and infrastructure development, human rights, psycho-social counselling, and community health promotion.

04

Economic Programmes for Livelihoods

Member-run savings and lending schemes, collective bargaining for inputs and produce, and enterprise skills that create income beyond subsistence farming.

05

Partnership & Resource Mobilisation

We partner with other actors and international donors to advance community development, reaching further as a registered group than individual efforts can alone.

06

Environmental Protection

We promote care for natural resources and practical actions that protect the land, water, and environment on which community livelihoods depend.

Our Story

From a room in Aojakitoi Parish to a growing community movement.

2026: The Beginning

Founded in Aojakitoi Parish

A group of community promoters came together around a shared conviction: the services Teso depends on are stretched beyond capacity. AIDI was constituted to help close that gap.

Today: Standing Alongside the Hard-to-Reach

Working in Kuju Subcounty and across Teso

Our approach begins with the community itself: identifying who is excluded, understanding why, and building the local structures that can carry services the last mile.

Tomorrow: Where We Are Going

Growing Savings, Agriculture, and Protection

We intend to grow our savings and lending scheme from a handful of members to a functioning community financial institution; expand our agricultural work from demonstration to scale; and build genuine protection capacity.

How We Are Governed

AIDI is governed by a Board of Executives of seven founder members, working jointly with a Top Management and Planning Committee. Day-to-day implementation is carried out by the Secretariat.

Board of Executives

Seven founder members. Supreme governing body. Holds charge of the Initiative's assets, resources, and income.

Top Management & Planning

Works jointly with the Board as the supreme decision-making body on all matters about the Initiative.

The Secretariat

The Initiative's implementing arm, carrying out day-to-day work in the community.

Staff Recruitment Commission

Oversees staffing, identifying gaps, advertising, receiving applications, and conducting interviews.

Board Meetings

Board meets in ordinary session at least twice a year. Financial year runs from 1st January to 31st December.

Audit & Accountability

Accounts subject to both internal audit and external audit nominated by the Top Management and Planning Committee and approved by the Board.

Our Reach

AIDI works across two regions in eastern and north-eastern Uganda. Our headquarters is in Aojakitoi Parish, Kuju Subcounty, Amuria District — a location that positions us to reach communities in both Teso and Karamoja that are often beyond the reach of mainstream service delivery.

We cooperate with Government and Local Government Authorities in the districts where we work, on the condition that AIDI retains administrative control over its own funds, personnel, and assets.

Ways to Give

Every contribution compounds. Here is how your support is used.

Savings & Lending Schemes

Help us establish member-run savings structures that keep capital circulating locally, member after member, season after season.

Agricultural Training

Fund demonstration plots and practical training in climate-resilient farming that withstands shocks and preserves land for future generations.

Education & Skills

Support adult literacy, vocational education, and school infrastructure — building the capacity of families to shape their own development.

What Your Support Enables

Practical, community-led outcomes — not promises.

01

Establish Member-Run Savings

Your gift seeds the capital pools that let community groups lend to one another, smoothing seasonal income and funding small enterprise — money that stays in the community and compounds.

02

Deliver Practical Skills Training

Fund vocational training, adult literacy, and enterprise skills sessions that give families the tools to earn beyond subsistence farming and build household resilience.

03

Equip Community Groups

Help us register, train, and connect community groups so they can access the government programmes, markets, and partnerships they have long been excluded from.

04

Scale Climate-Smart Agriculture

Support demonstration plots, drought-resistant seed, and practical training that help farmers weather climate shocks and preserve land for the next generation.
