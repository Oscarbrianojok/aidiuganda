Voices from the Community

"AIDI's constitution commits us to serve regardless of religion, tribe, colour, or race, and to work in partnership with communities rather than at a distance from them. Our approach begins with the community itself."
AIDI Founding Principle Aojakitoi Parish, Kuju Subcounty
"Infrastructure is not construction. It is access. A community that cannot reach a market cannot sell its harvest at a fair price; a community that cannot reach a road cannot reach a clinic."
AIDI Constitution Article on Infrastructure
"Support that compounds. Every shilling circulated through a structured local scheme keeps working, member after member, season after season."
AIDI Economic Vision Savings & Lending Schemes
